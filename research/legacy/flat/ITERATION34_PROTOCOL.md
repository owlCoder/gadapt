## Candidate scientific/engineering contribution

Add opt-in `mutation_operator_credit="censored_verified_per_cost"` directly to GAdapt `CreditBasedDecisionVariableMutator` while retaining actual GA, original old options/defaults, population/crossover. Require `mutation_operator_policy="ucb"` or `"uniform"`, `mutation_credit_assignment="offspring"`, `mutation_scenario_count=3`, a monotone actual-simulator counter and PURE cached callback `mutation_violation_function(values,penalized_score)`, returning a complete, last-evaluation-only dictionary:
- `{"status":"verified_feasible","actual_calls":3,"observed_scenarios":[unique scenario labels, all 3],"observed_violations":[0.,0.,0.]}`. Reward `1/actual_calls` (so 1/3), **only when all 3 real evaluations were performed and every measured constraint passed**.
- `{"status":"measured_infeasible","actual_calls":k,"observed_scenarios":[unique k],"observed_violations":[...≥0 with at least one >0]}`. Reward **0** regardless of `k=1,2,3`, and do not pretend any unobserved scenario feasible. This deliberately avoids encouraging cheap early rejections just because they are inexpensive; zero observations are tracked to update UCB's credit history.
- `{"status":"numerically_unknown",...}` or callback `None`: WITHHOLD reward and observation, not inferred failure/feasibility. Delta=0 post-budget: without feedback callback, withhold.
- Enforce `actual_calls==delta` at core true-counter boundary and `len(observed)==delta`, distinct labels, finite nonnegative violations, strict status consistency. Any inconsistent callback is fatal (fail-closed), no reward. `k` is **1..3** for a fully charged optimizer objective; partial k>0 now allowed in NEW mode, unlike old `scenario_feasibility` which still requires exactly N. Old UCB and old default behaviour unchanged. No new solver in callback; only read the latest already counted `SequentialScenarioBudgetCost` log.
