# Doctoral supervisor briefing (as of 2026-10-29, following E4, I64-I, I64-I2 and I63-D): GAdapt reliability, accuracy and applicability

Written for the doctoral supervisor to distinguish verified findings from unresolved claims.

## 1. The supervisor's question and what was done

The supervisor's assessment (as relayed by the student) was that GAdapt performs well, surpassing all algorithms but one while remaining more accurate; the objective is stability and practical industrial use. The experiment at `bpzoran/gadapt_experiments` (commit `0e388356`) was **replicated** with the mentor's functions and configurations: nine classical functions, 21 cells and a 20-generation stagnation rule, but with independent accounting of **actual objective-function calls**, explicit DE seeding and disclosure that three of the 20 seeds had already appeared in a development probe. I64-S0 comprised 1,680 runs, was frozen before measurement and independently numerically reproduced.

## 2. What S0 established

- **Evaluation efficiency (ECDF area through 12,000 calls):** GAdapt was ahead of all three tested competitors (PyGAD random/adaptive and SciPy DE). Simultaneous intervals support the frozen H64a decision. The A advantage over DE is small (+0.047 on a 0–1 scale), while the gaps against the PyGAD methods are larger (+0.16 to +0.20).
- **Final accuracy at each algorithm's original stopping point:** GAdapt has a lower weighted average of cell final medians of normalized gap, but much of the DE contrast comes from two difficult cells (Rastrigin-20 and Griewank-7). **SciPy DE has lower final error in 14 of 21 individual cells.** On smooth functions, DE can reach roughly 1e−14 while GAdapt typically reaches about 1e−5 to 1e−8. A post-hoc equal-budget analysis through 12,000 calls yields GAdapt:DE:tie cell-median wins = 10:10:1; the overall weighted contrast favors GAdapt because of high-gap cells.
- **Call accounting:** Generations are not a valid shared cost unit. GAdapt/PyGAD random/PyGAD adaptive use about 32/64/96 evaluations per generation, including repeated `best_solution()` evaluations for PyGAD. In 20 dimensions DE uses roughly 36,000 calls while GAdapt uses about 19,000. Generation counts therefore cannot be compared directly.
- **Limitations:** No shift or orthogonal transformation was applied; four functions have an optimum at the origin. Only 20 seeds were used. General accuracy superiority, industrial reliability and a causal role of mutation cannot be claimed.

## 3. Findings completed after S0

- **I64-E (shifted/rotated functions, 12,000 equal actual calls, nine configurations, 100 seeds):** frozen H64a was **opposite**. Simultaneous intervals favor CMA, IPOP and BIPOP over GAdapt for weighted A; GAdapt beats both PyGAD variants, DE and (1+1)-ES on A. Descriptively, GAdapt CVaR is 0.0033 on original and shifted functions but 0.0386 under Q (CMA family approximately 0.019). Axis alignment is not the same as separability and this does not establish performance on infrastructure problems. Preset H64b was not supported (CVaR 0.0117 → 0.0121).
- **I64-A (preregistered and independently reproduced hybrid ablation):** replacing the first 30% GAdapt phase of a GAdapt→CMA hybrid with random sampling, keeping CMA unchanged, reduces A by 0.131 [0.124, 0.139] under Q and 0.178 [0.174, 0.182] on original+shift, also worsening the tail. The GAdapt stage contributes in this fixed hybrid. The estimand is conditional on its σ₀ = 0.05, not evidence of universal superiority to random search.
- **I64-C (preregistered and independently reproduced mechanism study):** the hypothesis that Q homogenizes GAdapt's columnar-diversity coefficients was not confirmed (all three hypotheses undecided; rank association with A loss ρ = 0.27 versus trivial baseline ρ = 0.58). The mechanism remains unknown.
- **I64-R (preregistered and independently reproduced orthogonal-transformation control):** at matched shifts, Q reduces GAdapt-M weighted ECDF area by 0.081 [0.072, 0.090] and IPOP area by 0.010 [0.004, 0.017], difference 0.071 [0.060, 0.082]. Replacing the translation is negligible (+0.003 [−0.008, 0.014]). Thus the observed Q-related loss is not a shift artifact on the 21 tested cells/two methods; no mechanism is established.
- **I64-E2 (preregistered negative result):** opt-in differential crossover fails to close the Q gap; A decreases under Q and on combined original+shift. Its cause is not isolated.
- **I64-I / I64-I2:** Discrete ORPD with nine configurations, eight cells × 30 seeds and 12,000 actual power-flow evaluations. GAdapt feasibility (0.746) is among the strongest and is demonstrably better than PyGAD and DE, but differences with CMA-family methods are unresolved (H64c undecided); preset is opposite. Hybrid benefit relative to GAdapt-M is unconfirmed and its tail is worse than IPOP (H64h1 undecided; H64h2 opposite). **Provenance caveat:** `i_run_sha256` was empty in the I2 archive; I artifact hashes were supplied in an analysis-time addendum, so the required preregistered pre-run hash check was not met, although numerical results were independently reproduced. The coupling-index κ selection hypothesis remained undecided (5/8, 7/8).
- **I63:** Conceptual replication of an ORPD interaction stopped finally at its power stage because the frozen strict calibration rule failed. That STOP yields no test of H56a. Descriptive I63-D completed, θ₆₃ = 0.0139 with 95% interval [−0.0090, 0.0372], matching I56's sign but small and imprecise, **not** a confirmatory replication. The library had changed during execution; frozen-tree replay matched 128/128 tasks.
- **I64-E3 (30% GAdapt / 70% CMA-ES hybrid):** E3 improved the rotated outcomes versus GAdapt-M (H64g1 supported), but H64g2 was opposite (original+shift tail 1.8× larger) and H64g3 undecided, so the overall criterion failed. Post hoc, among six listed configurations the hybrid had the lowest aggregate CVaR and median gap (0.0070 and 0.00293) but A = 0.7511 versus 0.7699 for IPOP; noninferiority was not confirmed. This is **not standalone GAdapt**, since CMA uses most of the budget. The independent phase contribution was not identified within E3 itself without an ablation.

## 4. What has not been established

No original optimization method or theorem was found: 16 candidate directions were rejected based on checked prior art, including step control, signal invariance, penalty theory and bounded uniqueness. The current thesis material is replication and extension of the mentor's benchmark with actual-call accounting, execution-integrity audits (tool and studies), preregistered studies with both negative and inconclusive outcomes, and optional library features with implementation checks. General reliability, practical superiority and industrial readiness remain unproven. The supervisor must decide whether the available original contribution is sufficient for a doctorate.

2. Are classical functions without shifts/rotations an acceptable basis, or are transformed functions or real infrastructure tasks required?
3. Does "stability" mean small across-seed variance, absence of rare poor outcomes, or deterministic replay under the same seed? What numerical thresholds should be used?
