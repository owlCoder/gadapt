## Paper 1 — Evaluation Integrity

**Proposed placement:** End of archival verification addendum, after the I55 reproduction-level discussion.

> **I55 exact-count versus binary-flag reconciliation.** A follow-on, independent read of all matched I50/I55 per-cell JSON sources recovered 318 identical trace digests from 358 comparable cells; 40 were different (30 SMAC, 10 emukit), and two original I50 bofire cells had timed out and were excluded from the comparison. The exact numerical C/R flag counts differed in only one comparable cell: emukit on the plateau function at dimension 2, seed 6001, for which C1 changed from 2 to 3 and C2 changed from 0 to 1. The original frozen H55d mismatch count of one remains correct under its **Boolean flag-presence criterion**, since C1 was already present in both archives and only C2 changed from absent to present. Direct decoding of the I55 raw NPZ sequence at this cell reproduced C1=3 and C2=1; nine other archived emukit plateau NPZ traces also reproduced their C1–C5 counters. Four selected primary-process NPZ traces were independently rehashed and agreed with the saved I55 SHA-256 digests. These narrow checks do **not** constitute a raw replay of all 620 valid I55 primary-process traces. The stored NPZ files cover only the primary process A; the separate A′/B process sequence data were preserved as digests and sentinel metadata rather than complete raw NPZ call traces. Consequently, archived hash-seed control evidence can be checked for internal consistency but cannot be independently reconstructed at the three-process raw-byte level from these NPZ files alone. The strict original H55d decision remains **NOT SUPPORTED**.

**Mandatory editorial caution:** Do not write “all numerical flags except C2 agree” or “620 full three-process raw traces independently replayed.” Instead distinguish exact counters, binary labels, primary-A trace provenance and A′/B digest evidence.

## Paper 2 — Context-Dependent GAdapt

**Proposed placement:** After the existing I64 source-verification addendum.

> **Independent archived-analysis arithmetic recheck.** A separate source-level audit decoded seven original compressed I64 analysis JSON artifacts (S0, E, R, E3, A, I and I2), retained their Git blob identities and independently recalculated 41 aggregate/contrast/direction consistency conditions; all 41 passed. In S0's limited original-comparator configuration, the archived ECDF areas were 0.6983036 for GAdapt and 0.6509896 for DE. An independent read of all 21 S0 descriptive records confirmed that GAdapt had larger per-cell area A in 17/21 cells (DE 4/21), but **DE had lower original-stop final cell median gap in 14/21** (GAdapt 6/21, one tie). These are distinct measured quantities, not competing estimates of one common end-state superiority criterion. Under E's stronger equal-call comparison, the area difference GAdapt−IPOP was −0.0966628, with an entirely negative original simultaneous interval, preserving E's **opposite** verdict. In the fixed matched-shift R analysis, the differential loss attributable to the added orthogonal Q was 0.0707918 with archived simultaneous interval [0.0598119, 0.0817717], conditional on the exact tested functions, dimensions and Q matrices. The E3 hybrid improved the rotated ECDF contrast but failed the prescribed nonrotated tail criterion (1.5×CVaR(M)−CVaR(H)=−0.00069643, original interval [−0.00110910, −0.00028375]); its full hypothesis family was not supported. In discrete ORPD study I2, CVaR(IPOP)−CVaR(H)=−0.1253068, original interval [−0.2502239, −0.0003896], disfavouring the hybrid in tail loss. The separate feasibility-proportion difference P(H)−P(IPOP)=−0.0291667 has an original interval [−0.0642794, 0.0059461] spanning zero, so a strict feasibility-loss finding is not independently established. These checks confirm arithmetic against original archived **analysis aggregates** only: individual-run bootstrap and stochastic optimizer executions were **not** recomputed. None of the findings supports universal superiority, field-network validity or a novel algorithmic policy.

**Mandatory editorial caution:** Do not convert the original conditional R effect to “rotation always harms GAdapt more,” conflate the S0 and E designs, interpret E3's rotated gain as overall hybrid support, or conflate ORPD feasibility with tail-CVaR significance.
