## Manuscript B — Context-dependent GAdapt (proposed addition after I64-R orthogonal-contrast results)

> **Sensitivity to benchmark composition (exploratory).** The prespecified I64-R four-contrast analysis indicated that, for the fixed 21 function–dimension cells, orthogonal transformations (including possible reflections) lowered the anytime ECDF area of GAdapt-M at least 0.05 more than that of IPOP-CMA-ES (c4 = 0.07079; simultaneous 95% CI [0.05981, 0.08177], computed under seed-only uncertainty). An additional post hoc leave-one-objective-family-out calculation, retaining the original per-family weighting rule among remaining families, yielded a c4 point estimate of **0.04087** after removing the Rastrigin family, compared with 0.07079 on all nine families. Removing Styblinski–Tang yielded 0.05575; the other family exclusions gave c4 point estimates between 0.07028 and 0.08221. Because no leave-one-family-out bootstrap intervals were computed, these numbers describe estimand sensitivity and do not change the registered H64r3 decision. The seed bootstrap does not cover uncertainty about the benchmark family mix or the specific fixed orthogonal matrices.

## Manuscript B — Context-dependent GAdapt (proposed addition after I64-I2 ORPD results)

> **Concentration of the unfavorable ORPD tail contrast (exploratory).** Independently reconstructing per-cell CVaR₀,₉ and feasibility from the original eight I64-I and eight I64-I2 compressed per-cell records reproduced all four prespecified I64-I2 point contrasts. The full-sample tail contrast CVaR(IPOP)−CVaR(hybrid) was **−0.12530676**, and the original simultaneous interval excludes zero on the negative side, so H64h2 retains its registered *opposite* label. However, **case39/j=3** alone contributed **−0.125** of that mean: IPOP found feasible results in 30/30 runs, versus 27/30 for the hybrid; the hybrid's three infeasible outcomes saturated its worst-decile CVaR at 1, whereas IPOP's CVaR was 0. The three identified hybrid seeds were `68003015`, `68003018`, and `68003028`. Across the seven other cells, the renormalized tail contrast was **−0.00035058** (point estimate only). This is not a revised statistical decision but a descriptive influence analysis demonstrating that the tail deterioration on these eight cells was highly concentrated. A further case (`case39/j=4`) had saturated CVaR=1 for all three algorithms despite feasibility counts of 0/30 for GAdapt-M, 1/30 for the hybrid, and 5/30 for IPOP. Both feasibility and tail loss should therefore be displayed to prevent false impressions of uniform deterioration or equivalence.

## Manuscript A — Evaluation integrity (cross-manuscript methodological implication)

> **Distinguishing reproducibility estimands.** In a separate frozen I55 evaluation-integrity audit, archive-level equality of complete call-sequence digests (I50 versus I55) was 318 of 358 comparable cells, while equality of detector-presence indicators held in 357 of 358. These are different properties. The sole cell with a changed presence indicator was `emukit/plateau/d=2/seed=6001` (C2: 0→1); it also changed the raw C1 count from 2→3, without changing C1's presence indicator. This illustrates why byte-level reproducibility, count-level detector stability and binary flagged/unflagged stability must be reported distinctly. The emukit thread-setting explanation is supported by the historical post hoc diagnostic and should not be represented as a newly randomized mechanism experiment.
