# Doctoral supervisor meeting notes (Codex 093 and 097, 2026-10-29; I63-D completed 2026-10-29)

**Strongest presently defensible contribution statement:** the dissertation provides a novel but **limited empirical characterization** of GAdapt and the reliability of its comparison to alternative optimizers. Instrumented replication and preregistered extensions of the mentor's benchmark separate efficiency by actual evaluation calls, final accuracy and tail risk, demonstrating that rankings depend on the metric and problem transformations. Controlled ORPD studies reveal a localized interaction between mutation/adaptation policy and constraint handling, with documented transfer boundaries. In addition, an execution-integrity audit tool and its diagnostic and negative results are presented. The claim is **not** a new optimization method, theorem or priority for general evaluation procedures.

**Claims not permitted:** universally higher accuracy/stability; "beats all except one" as an established general fact; industrial readiness; rotation or failure to learn correlations as an isolated cause; κ as a validated selector; H56a as replicated; originality of differential crossover, the hybrid or compensatory mutation; or, at the time of the initial notes, the individual contribution of the GAdapt stage inside the hybrid.
