## Actual evidence and detector uncertainty: protect both positives and negatives

1. **I49 synthetic detector:** one preregistered run of 10 libraries (nine eligible for the published prevalence denominator), 6 problems and 5 seeds. The declared primary threshold found 4/9 libraries with flags (Clopper–Pearson 95% interval [0.14,0.79]); **all four individual issues were previously known**. Of these, DEAP boundary handling is a documented design decision and PyGAD post-return reevaluation was documented; only two of nine contradicted documentation. Therefore **4/9 is not a measured prevalence of undisclosed software bugs**.
2. **I50 independent library roster:** 11/12 libraries had a flagged condition if ambiguous C1 is included; **excluding C1 gives 4/12**, a sensitivity (post hoc) that would change the preregistered prevalence decision to undecided. C1 is frequently a legitimate endpoint/corner proposal rather than a hidden validation call. Predeclared documentation-contradiction rule identified **2/12** (GFO R2 and SMAC D3x), both reported to maintainers. This is the **principal method validity stress test**: an accuracy claim cannot ignore C1's false positive mechanism.
3. **I52 AC-dispatch transfer:** original preregistered yes/no status *excluding C1* was concordant in **19/21** libraries with the synthetic study, with independent case14/case39/case118 tests and a separate frozen environmental failure before any objective call. The real AC simulation additionally exposed a SciPy DE reported-vs-evaluated-best discrepancy under polishing (R3 in 5/15); scikit-opt DE R3 replicated. **This is a solver-computed engineering benchmark, NOT validated field power-grid measurements.**
4. **I55 replay of distinct hash seeds and raw traces:** a corrected control verified the hash-seed manipulation in **620/620 valid cells**, with **10 invalid Nevergrad cells**. ## Specific falsifiable methodological contribution for review — NOT yet a new result

**Question:** Can a *phase-aware, wrapper-and-solver-visible trace* identify objective-calling, reported-incumbent, failure and feasibility anomalies with adequate sensitivity and specificity, across optimization implementations and a separately solved AC simulator, under a fixed transparent **resource vector** rather than a misleading scalar budget?

**Proposed testable contribution:** explicit **truth-condition and evidence requirements** for claims made by a black-box optimizer, not a new evolutionary search policy: `returned best` must be observed and independently rechecked; `budget <= B` must identify *which* resource boundary and count failed/initialization/witness entries; `physically feasible` must pass independently computed AC model constraints; `reproducible` must identify hash-seed, BLAS-thread, source/environment and full call-sequence evidence. These ideas individually exist in the literature; the originality of a *combined, generalizable instrument and its validated specificity* remains **TO_BE_REVIEWED**, not established.

**Assumptions:** sequential/atomic reservation for attempts; complete interception of wrapped and nested solver entrypoints; exact source/model case hashes; no hidden aliases/concurrent subprocesses; independent feasibility rules on supported bus/gen topologies. Without these, report `COVERAGE_INCOMPLETE` and do not certify budgets or feasible dispatch.

**Simple falsifier (not a new theorem):** two hidden objective wrappers can return identical scalar `f(x)` for all observed x while one calls PF once and the other calls PF twice; an outside observer of only `f(x)` and the wrapper-attempt count cannot distinguish the physical-call costs. Thus **you cannot infer a fully accounted physical budget from objective-call logs alone**. This is an elementary non-identifiability construction, not a new complexity theorem.

**Strong baselines for a *future independently approved validity study*:** raw wrapper-only objective counter; unmodified I49 C1 detector; I50 corrected non-C1 classifier; a source-aware instrument with explicit phase labels; independent I56/PYPOWER verifier; and a code-independent Newton/dispatch verifier. Use blind adjudicated labels from synthetic mutants plus legitimately generated corner proposals to estimate **sensitivity AND false positive rate**; do not assume corner = probe or independent numerical software = physical grid truth.

**Prospective failure conditions:** unapproved writes to frozen studies, bad source hashes, trace omission, false C1 alarms from legitimate points, missing nested PF calls, false feasible label for the observed reactive-infeasible midpoint, swapped/crossed or uncharged witnesses, or a detector only working after excluded cases. A study cannot be called successful by ignoring its original negative/STOP outcomes.

**Prospective cost:** zero new evaluations in current review. A new approved validity experiment would be *small synthetic-only first* (fixed mutants, unmutated controls and blinded manual labels), then one development-only solver/case validation. Each physical PF/OPF effort requires independent cap and signed study protocol. Held-out COCO f21/f24 remains `NOT_RUN`.
