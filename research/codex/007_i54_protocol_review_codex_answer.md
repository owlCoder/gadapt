1. **Blocking: the y-box cancels the intended scales** ([protocol:19](/Users/danijel/Desktop/gadapt/research/ITERATION54_PROTOCOL.md:19)). Write \(s_i=\sum_j|B_{ji}|\). Then \(2h_i=s_i/d_i\), so GAdapt’s range-normalized coordinates see \(A\,\mathrm{diag}(2h)=B\,\mathrm{diag}(s)\): the eigenvalues disappear from initialization, resets and range-scaled mutation. For WD, this map is exactly identity. However, `log_relative` uses `log(1 + value − lower)`, so residual differences arise from coordinate units; WD is therefore not a clean scales control. **Fix:** decouple proposal scales from containing-box widths, use dimensionless diversity and matched initialization, and verify the effective proposal covariance before measuring performance.

2. **Blocking: clipping confounds geometry with boundary sampling** ([protocol:20](/Users/danijel/Desktop/gadapt/research/ITERATION54_PROTOCOL.md:20)). The enclosing y-box contains points outside the true transformed domain. Clipping creates probability mass on faces/corners, duplicate evaluations and flat regions containing distinct genotypes with identical phenotypes. This can help boundary solutions or waste most of the budget; reporting clipping counts cannot distinguish those explanations. **Fix:** initialize all branches from the same uniform u-populations, specify a common repair rule in u, and preregister a second boundary-handling sensitivity. Record clipped fraction, displacement and unique evaluated phenotypes. Require robustness before attributing gains to geometry.

3. **Failure cannot refute geometry learning generally** ([protocol:36](/Users/danijel/Desktop/gadapt/research/ITERATION54_PROTOCOL.md:36)). A terminal covariance after 60,000 evaluations describes a local search distribution, potentially dominated by active constraints or numerical stagnation. It need not help a 3,000-evaluation search starting far away. **Fix:** narrow rejection to “this fixed terminal-shape intervention was insufficient.” Preregister additional covariance checkpoints spanning early and late search, with a fixed eigenvalue-regularization rule; do not select checkpoints by I54 outcomes.

Exporting `es.C` alone may therefore supply the wrong shape. **Fix:** define whether the oracle is latent sampling geometry or physical-coordinate geometry; compose applicable linear decoding/scaling, freshly diagonalize the saved matrix, and archive its condition number and reconstruction checks.
